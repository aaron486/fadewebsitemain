05 · Our Offerings

What we offer sportsbooks.
01

Affiliate partners at scale

Fade turns its entire user base into an affiliate network — two revenue lines off one audience. Fade drives new users — CPA. Fade drives bets — NGR.

CPA

Drive new users to DraftKings

We sign up new users for you. Fade drives first-time sign-ups straight into DraftKings — and with DraftKings Predictions launching in all 50 states, every Fade user is an addressable acquisition. You pay on players who convert and fund.

NGR

Drive direct bets to DraftKings

Fade users export their picks straight to the book, so the handle lands with DraftKings and Fade is paid affiliate NGR on the net revenue those bets generate.

Promos

In-app promos and bonuses

Fade surfaces DraftKings deals, promos and free bets natively in the app — putting your offers in front of bettors at the moment they are deciding where to place the bet.

02

Talent curation & brand partnerships

Fade's deep partnership background lets sportsbooks activate with any influential voice in sports and media — from the world's biggest celebrities to micro betting influencers.

How our talent model works ↓

Fade does not sign or manage talent. We sit over the ecosystem rather than inside it — no roster to sell through, no exclusivities to work around.

That means we cast for the campaign, not for our own books. Fade brings partnerships to any agency, celebrity or brand, curated to what the activation actually needs.

03

UGC at scale

An army of 100–150 micro betting influencers floods the timeline with reaction content and UGC — every post driving back to CPA.

02

06

Brand protection & IP

Fade is built gambling-first. Everything we deliver is produced against CFTC and state gambling regulations — and against each platform's own digital guidelines, so framing, safe areas and mandatory disclosures survive the cut.

When framing guidelines are missed ↓

Creative cut for one aspect ratio and posted to another loses its edges to the platform's own UI. In both of these the responsible-gambling disclosure — the copy that has to stay legible — is the copy competing with the chrome.

Fade builds to each platform's safe areas from the storyboard on, so the disclosure never lands under the CTA.
