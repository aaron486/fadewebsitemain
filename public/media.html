Why a SAFE

SAFEs were 84% of early-stage convertible instruments in 2025 deal data; cap-or-discount (not both) is now standard. 2025 median SAFE cap: $10M.

Built like a fundable company

  • Clean instrument — standard post-money SAFE, the instrument in 84% of 2025 early-stage convertible deals. No side letters, no non-standard terms, cap-only pricing.
  • Regulated-space legal budget — year one budgeted at $35–60K: formation, IP assignment, and SAFE docs at market rate, plus state affiliate registrations and app-store gambling compliance.
  • Talent equity discipline — celebrity and advisor equity issued as performance-vested options from a capped pool (2–5% total; 0.25–1% per advisor) — never direct stock grants.
  • IP & cap table hygiene — all founder IP assigned at formation; restricted stock with vesting; dilution modeled through Series A before this round opened.
